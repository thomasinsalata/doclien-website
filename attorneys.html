What changes for your team.

Paralegals and case managers spend hours confirming appointments and requesting records. DocLien replaces that with one shared timeline per case.

Without DocLien

  • Calling around to find a provider who takes liens
  • Phone tag to confirm appointments
  • Authorizations and LOPs by fax and email
  • Asking each provider for treatment status
  • Chasing billing totals before demand

With DocLien

  • We assign a provider near your client
  • Appointments and reminders appear on the case
  • Documents are e-signed and filed automatically
  • Treatment status is visible any time
  • Billing reports are ready when you need them

What your firm uses every day.

Fast referrals

Submit a referral in the app or call our team. Either way it lands on a case right away.

Provider matching

We assign a provider based on the care your client needs, where they live and who can see them soonest.

Appointment visibility

See booked, completed and missed appointments for every client without a single call.

Documents and e-signatures

Treatment and billing reports
