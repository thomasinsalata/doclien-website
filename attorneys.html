What changes for your team.

Paralegals and case managers spend hours confirming appointments and requesting records. DocLien replaces that with one shared timeline per case.

Without DocLien

  • Phone tag to book and confirm appointments
  • Authorizations by fax and email
  • Asking each provider for treatment status
  • Chasing billing totals before settlement
  • Spreadsheets to track the caseload

With DocLien

  • Book from provider availability in real time

  • Billing reports ready when you need them
  • Every case in one dashboard
