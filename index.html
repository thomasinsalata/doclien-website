Questions firms and providers ask.

What does DocLien do?

DocLien coordinates medical care for personal-injury clients on behalf of their law firms. We match each client with a treating provider in our California network, book the appointments, collect signed authorizations, track treatment and deliver records and billing reports to counsel, all in one secure workspace.

Who chooses the provider?

DocLien does. When a firm sends a referral, our team assigns a provider based on the care the client needs, where the client lives and who has availability. Firms see the match and the appointment as soon as it is booked.

What is a Letter of Protection?

A Letter of Protection (LOP) lets a client receive treatment now, with the provider paid from the proceeds of the case settlement. DocLien prepares and administers the LOP paperwork between the firm, the patient and the provider.

Where does DocLien operate?

DocLien serves personal-injury clients across California, not just one metro area. Our provider network covers clients statewide.

How do providers join the network?

Request access as a medical provider and tell us your specialty and locations. Once your account is set up, you publish open appointment slots and start receiving referred patients with signed paperwork attached.

Do patients use DocLien too?

Yes. Patients have their own access to sign authorizations, confirm appointments and receive reminders. They only see their own appointments and documents.

Does DocLien give medical or legal advice?

No. DocLien provides coordination and administrative support only. Clinical decisions are made independently by treating providers, and legal advice comes from the client's attorney.
