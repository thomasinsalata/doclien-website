Common questions.

What is DocLien?

DocLien is a case-coordination platform for personal-injury law firms and medical providers. Scheduling, document exchange, e-signatures, treatment tracking and reports all live in one secure workspace.

Who is DocLien for?

Law firms that handle personal-injury cases, and the medical providers who treat their clients. Patients also get their own access to sign documents and receive reminders.

How does scheduling work?

Providers publish their open appointment slots. Law firms book and manage appointments directly from that availability, and everyone sees the same calendar.

Can documents be signed electronically?

Yes. E-signatures are built in, so authorizations and case documents can be sent, signed and stored on the case without printing or faxing.

Is our data secure?

DocLien handles protected health information under HIPAA. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-based, and activity is logged for every case.

How do we get started?

Request access and tell us about your firm or practice. Our team will set up your account and add your users.

Does DocLien give medical or legal advice?

No. DocLien provides coordination and administrative tools only. Clinical decisions are made by treating providers, and legal advice comes from the client's attorney.
