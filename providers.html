What changes for your front desk.

Lien patients come with extra coordination: attorneys to update, documents to collect, bills to send. DocLien takes that work off your staff.

Without DocLien

  • Empty slots you hope get filled
  • Patients arriving without signed paperwork
  • Calls from attorneys asking for status
  • Faxing records and bills one by one
  • No-shows nobody followed up on

With DocLien

  • Referred patients booked into your open slots
  • Authorization and LOP signed before the visit
  • Counsel sees status on the shared timeline
  • Records and bills sent securely in one step
  • Automatic reminders before every appointment

What your practice uses every day.

Publish availability

Post your open appointment slots. DocLien books referred patients into them, and our team can also book by phone.

Signed documents up front

Receive the patient's HIPAA authorization and Letter of Protection electronically, filed to the case.

Fewer no-shows

Patients get automatic reminders, and missed visits are flagged to DocLien and the firm.

Treatment updates

Share progress on a timeline the referring firm can see, instead of answering status calls.

Billing to counsel

Send itemized billing and records securely so the lien is documented for settlement.

Access for your staff

Front desk, billing and clinical staff each get their own login and the access their role needs.
